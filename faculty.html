Faculty & departments

Every number here leaves out papers with 100 or more authors (see Big collaborations). Papers with 11–99 authors stay in and are counted in the ">10 authors" column.

How do departments compare?

Departments differ in how much and where their fields publish. Mathematics writes fewer, longer papers, and its journals have lower citation rates, so its impact-factor shares are lower by nature. Compare a department with itself across the two institutes, not with other fields.

How it's computed
  • A paper counts once in every department that has an author on it.
  • Q1 share = Q1 papers ÷ journal papers whose venue SCImago ranks.
  • Q1 & IF>5 adds a second condition: the journal's SCImago citations per document over two years (our open stand-in for the impact factor) is above 5.
  • Median h is the median of the faculty members' h-index over 2016–2026 papers.

How it's computed

h-index (2016–26): the largest h such that h of the person's papers in this dataset have at least h citations each. Career h-index is OpenAlex's value over the whole career, including papers before 2016, at other institutions and with 100+ authors, so the two are not comparable.
