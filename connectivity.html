Whose work do we cite?

"No Indian author, or unknown" is a remainder: total citations (or references) minus the Indian ones we fetched. It includes papers whose affiliations OpenAlex has not parsed. The incoming and outgoing shares are comparable, but raw counts are not: references can point to work of any year, while citations can only reach our 2016–26 papers.

How it's computed

Every citation link goes into the first category that fits:

  1. Self-citation: the two papers share an author, whether or not the citing paper is one of ours.
  2. Same IISER: the other paper has an author at the same institute, but no shared author.
  3. Indian and foreign authors: the other paper has both. Papers with 100+ authors also go here, since their author lists are truncated.
  4. Other Indian institutes only: all of the other paper's authors are in India. For Pune this includes IISER Kolkata, and the other way round.
  5. No Indian author, or unknown: everything else. OpenAlex gives the total, and we subtract the Indian links.

Fractional counting is a check on the categories. If each citing paper is split by the share of its authors who are in India, India accounts for , self-citations included.

How it's computed

For each institution we take the OpenAlex subfields that make up at least 0.5% of its 2016–26 papers. The Jaccard similarity of two institutions is the number of subfields they share divided by the number either has.
