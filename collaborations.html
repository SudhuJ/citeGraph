Big collaborations (100+ authors)

Particle-physics and gravitational-wave experiments publish papers with hundreds or thousands of authors. OpenAlex cuts author lists at 100, so we cannot check who on these papers was at an IISER, and one person's paper count can run into the hundreds. We keep these papers in the deliverable CSVs, flagged hyperauthored, and leave them out of every other page. This page shows what they are and what they would change.
