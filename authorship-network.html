Authorship network

Undirected co-authorship graphs. The instruction note defines nodes as "faculty members" (§6b) while the slides say "authors", so we build both and compare them. G_fac: our publishing faculty only, linked when two of them co-author. G_ind: faculty plus their Indian co-authors at other institutions. Edge weight = Newman's collaboration strength Σ 1/(n−1) over shared papers. Papers with ≥100 authors are left out of both graphs (see sensitivity below).

Faculty centrality

Degree centrality = most collaborative; betweenness (shortest paths with distance 1/strength) = brokers between groups. Both shown for the two graphs: a faculty member can be peripheral among IISER colleagues yet central in the wider Indian network.
